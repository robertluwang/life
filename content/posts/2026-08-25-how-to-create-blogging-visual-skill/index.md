The linework had to be wobbly, shaky, loose, and of slightly varied weight, mimicking a real felt-tip pen drawn in a single breath. For colors, I specified a "Lazy Offset" style where the color fill does not align with the line boundaries, recreating a misregistered screen-print error. The palette was restricted entirely to desaturated Morandi tones like dusty blue, sage green, cocoa brown, and aged creams, banning primary colors.'
